# Broad Project Concept

**Status:** Exploratory
**Stage:** Initial Planning

## Project Idea

Develop a **wireless safety system using low-power wireless communication and embedded electronics**.

The system will investigate how wireless technology can be used to help people **communicate safety information, alerts, or status information without relying entirely on conventional communication infrastructure**.

One area of interest is using **LoRa-based technology and Meshtastic networks**, including the possibility of making use of existing Meshtastic infrastructure.

The exact application, hardware, software, and features have not yet been decided.

---

## Areas Being Explored

The project may involve:

* Embedded electronics
* Wireless communication
* LoRa / LoRa-based technology
* Meshtastic
* Sensors
* Safety alerts
* Low-power operation
* Battery-powered hardware
* Local user interaction
* Wireless networking
* Data communication
* Reliability and range testing

---

## General Goal

The general goal is to investigate whether **low-power wireless and embedded technology can provide a practical safety-related system**, and to design, build and test a working prototype.

---

## Important Open Questions

The project still needs to determine:

* What specific safety problem to address
* Who the system is intended for
* What information needs to be communicated
* How the information should be detected or generated
* How the information should be transmitted
* Whether existing Meshtastic networks can be used
* What hardware is appropriate
* What level of reliability is required
* What can realistically be completed within the final-year project

---

## Current Technical Interest

**LoRa / Meshtastic** is currently an important technical direction, but the final communication architecture has not been approved.

Other technologies may be investigated if research shows that they are more appropriate for the eventual requirements.

---

## Project Status

This is an **idea and research direction**, not a final specification.

No Feature Version or Master functionality has been approved.
